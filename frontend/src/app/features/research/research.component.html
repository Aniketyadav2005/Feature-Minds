📖 What do these metrics mean?
Accuracy

Percentage of all predictions that were correct.

Precision

Of all images flagged as hateful, how many actually were?

Recall

Of all actually hateful images, how many did the model catch?

F1 Score

Harmonic mean of Precision and Recall.
